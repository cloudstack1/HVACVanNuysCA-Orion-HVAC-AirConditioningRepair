Orion HVAC is a full-service heating and air conditioning company serving residential and commercial customers across the San Fernando Valley for 8 years. Our Van Nuys office at 15922 Strathern St #20 keeps our technicians close to the neighborhoods that call us most.

Van Nuys sits in the middle of the Valley, where summer temperatures regularly pass 100°F and air conditioners run for hours without a break. Many homes here were built in the 1950s and 1960s with ductwork in hot attics and older equipment that was never designed for today's heat waves. That wears out capacitors, motors and compressors faster than in coastal Los Angeles, and our technicians know what to check first.
